Task 1:
Create an AI Research Agent using n8n that:
Collects trending information from Twitter (X) and Reddit
Summarizes the research into a one-page newsletter
Includes one relevant image
Formats the content neatly and clearly
Outcome: Sends the newsletter to Slack and Gmail


Task 2 :
Use the same AI Research Agent from Task 1 and extend it as follows:
1.     Convert the workflow to run on a scheduled trigger (daily).
2.     Add a webhook to handle user subscriptions.
3.     Convert the solution into a Micro-SaaS using Lovable or Google AI Studio.
4.     When a user subscribes with their email:
o   Send an immediate welcome email
o   Automatically send the AI-generated newsletter on a daily basis
5.     Ensure the daily newsletter is delivered via email (and optionally Slack, if configured).
Outcome:
A simple Micro-SaaS where users subscribe with their email and receive a daily AI-generated newsletter automatically.
